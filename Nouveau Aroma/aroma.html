OVERVIEW

Role

UX Research, Design, Prototyping

Tools

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Figma

Date

2023

Nouveau Aroma’s digital transformation was inspired by a desire to create a refined, immersive experience for fragrance enthusiasts worldwide. The vision was to translate the brand's rich heritage of artisanal craftsmanship into a contemporary, luxurious digital space. The project’s goal was to fuse elegance with usability, ensuring that the essence of French perfumery was communicated through every interaction.

PROJECT GENESIS

Timeline

This project unfolded over a carefully structured 17-week period. Each phase was meticulously planned, starting with deep discovery and research, moving through design iterations, and culminating in a fully responsive, high-fidelity prototype that was ready for launch.

INSIGHT DISCOVERY

User Research Findings

Our research revealed diverse preferences among luxury fragrance shoppers, leading to key insights that shaped the design:

  • Ease of Navigation (45%): A significant number of users value simple and intuitive website navigation that enables effortless exploration of products.
  • Story-Driven Experience (35%): Users are drawn to storytelling that conveys the brand's history, allowing them to emotionally connect with the product.
  • Mobile Shopping (20%): A portion of luxury consumers primarily browse and make purchases through their smartphones, emphasizing the need for seamless mobile design.

Competitor Analysis

  • Dior: Emphasizes elegance with sleek, high-end design.
  • Chanel: Focuses on minimalism and exclusivity.
  • Jo Malone: Stands out with personalization and emotional connection.

Key Point

  • Pricing: Dior and Chanel are high-end; Jo Malone is more accessible.
  • User Experience: Dior and Chanel focus on sophistication, while Jo Malone prioritizes personalization.
  • Marketing: Dior uses celebrities, Chanel relies on prestige, and Jo Malone engages customers with influencers and customization.

DESIGN PROCESS

Information Architecture

We began by carefully structuring the information architecture to ensure clarity and ease of use. The sitemap was designed to guide users through the journey of exploring fragrances, learning about the brand’s heritage, and making informed purchase decisions. Every page was strategically placed to create a flow that felt intuitive and luxurious, from the homepage to individual product pages and beyond.

User Flow

The user flow was designed with simplicity and elegance in mind. Our goal was to ensure that users could easily navigate the site, whether they were browsing through the fragrance collection, reading about the brand’s history, or completing a purchase. We mapped out clear pathways to ensure that the user journey was fluid, engaging, and consistent with the brand’s upscale feel.

VISUAL AESTHETIC

Typography

The typography was thoughtfully chosen to enhance the brand’s luxurious yet approachable aesthetic. We paired Fraunces, a classic serif font, with Poppins, a clean and modern sans-serif, to create a refined and balanced visual identity. Fraunces adds a touch of tradition and elegance, while Poppins brings a contemporary flair, ensuring clarity and sophistication throughout the design. This combination not only adds depth to the typography but also guides users seamlessly through the site, enhancing the overall user experience with a blend of elegance and modernity.

Colour Palette

The color palette was inspired by Nouveau Aroma’s brand identity—elegant, timeless, and luxurious. We chose a refined combination of black, white, and gold to evoke a sense of sophistication. Black conveyed mystery and depth, white added purity and simplicity, and gold symbolized the brand’s premium nature. Together, these colors created a harmonious aesthetic that reflected the artisanal quality of the fragrances.

Design

The high-fidelity mockups showcase the elegance of Nouveau Aroma through full-screen product images, smooth scroll animations, and delicate hover effects on perfume bottles. Each section of the website is crafted to evoke the luxurious and sophisticated essence of the brand. From the moment users land on the homepage, they are greeted with vibrant, high-resolution imagery that mirrors the in-store experience, allowing them to feel as though they are browsing the perfume collection in person.

The larger screen format allows for a more extensive presentation of product features and storytelling elements, enabling users to engage deeply with the fragrances they are considering. Each mockup effectively illustrates how customers can access rich content, including fragrance notes, application tips, and insights into the brand's heritage, all of which enhance the shopping experience.

INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

Prototype

This section provides an engaging and hands-on look at the interactive prototype of Nouveau Aroma. Through seamless transitions and thoughtful animations, users are invited to explore the essence of the brand through a fluid online experience that mirrors an in-store visit.

Key Features of the Interactive Experience

1. Seamless Bottle Animation: Perfume bottles elegantly descend from the landing page during the loading sequence, creating a visually captivating introduction to the website.

2. Intuitive Product Exploration: Users can easily navigate between the shop page, individual product pages, and their cart with smooth transitions and interactive hover effects.

3. Elegant Cart Interaction: A sleek popup appears when adding items to the cart, allowing users to view and manage their selections without disrupting the shopping flow.

4. Refined Checkout Process: The checkout page is streamlined for a luxurious experience, featuring clean design elements, progress indicators, and easy-to-follow forms for a seamless transaction.

CREATIVE SOLUTIONS

Design Challenges

One of the key challenges we faced during the design process was balancing the need for a visually rich, luxurious design with the demand for fast-loading, responsive pages in Figma. The homepage animation was initially designed to be visually engaging but encountered performance issues within the prototype. The animations lagged or misaligned, particularly when handling complex sequences and high-resolution images. These problems disrupted the intended smoothness and consistency of the user journey in the prototype. Additionally, the use of high-quality imagery and detailed descriptions required careful optimization to ensure users could enjoy the experience without delays.

Another significant challenge arose when attempting to replicate the same animation from the desktop homepage on the mobile homepage. Due to the constraints of mobile performance and screen size, it became apparent that the intricate animations would not translate effectively. As a compromise, I decided to simplify the mobile experience by removing the bottle animation entirely, which allowed us to prioritize usability without sacrificing the overall elegance of the design.

Problem Solving

To address these challenges, we implemented several solutions:

  • High-Quality Image Optimization: We compressed images while preserving their luxurious look, ensuring that the design remained visually appealing without slowing down performance. This optimization allowed for faster loading times while maintaining image integrity.
  • Simplified Animations: By reducing the complexity of the animations on the homepage, we ensured smoother transitions and eliminated the lag that was disrupting the user experience. This simplification helped to create a more seamless interaction for users.
  • Smart Loading of Visuals: A lazy-loading strategy was implemented to prioritize key visuals, allowing the most important elements to load immediately while less critical content loaded in the background. This approach enhanced the overall efficiency of the page.
  • These solutions allowed us to maintain the luxurious aesthetic of Nouveau Aroma while ensuring that the prototype remained fluid, responsive, and efficient.
