Tools

Manqanebi.ge was designed to revolutionize the way users find and manage property maintenance services, offering an easy-to-use and efficient platform for all their home repair needs. The vision was to create a seamless, intuitive experience where users could quickly discover, request, and monitor the status of their repair services with ease. The project aimed to blend convenience with smart functionality, ensuring that every interaction simplified the maintenance process while delivering reliable, top-notch service to users.

INSIGHT DISCOVERY

User Research Findings

While research I uncovered key user preferences that guided the design of Manqanebi.ge:

  • Ease of Navigation: Users want an intuitive platform that allows them to easily browse and filter car listings, helping them find their desired vehicle quickly.
  • Detailed Information: Many users seek detailed information about cars, including specs, condition, and history, ensuring they can make informed purchase decisions.
  • Visual Appeal: High-quality images and videos of cars are essential, as users want to view the vehicles in detail before making contact.
  • Price Transparency: Users value clear and transparent pricing, wanting to see the price upfront without hidden fees or complex negotiations.

Competitor Analysis

By evaluating competitors in the property maintenance space, we identified several areas for improvement:

  • Autotrader provides a wide range of listings but lacks local focus and a user-friendly search function.
  • Carvana offers an easy buying process but is limited to certain areas and doesn’t provide enough car variety.
  • Cars24 offers great deals but often has limited information about the car’s full history, which can deter potential buyers.

DESIGN PROCESS

Information Architecture

We began by carefully structuring the information architecture to ensure a smooth and user-friendly experience on Manqanebi.ge. The sitemap was designed to guide users through browsing car listings, learning about vehicle details, and making confident purchasing decisions. Each section was strategically placed to create a seamless flow, from the homepage to individual car pages, allowing users to easily navigate and find the car they desired. The structure was built with simplicity and efficiency in mind, ensuring an intuitive, responsive experience at every step.
