OVERVIEW

Role

UX Research, Design, Prototyping

Tools

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Figma

Date

2023

Coupons Found was designed to revolutionise the way users discover and utilise discounts, making savings effortless and accessible. The vision was to create a seamless, intuitive platform where users can effortlessly browse, save, and redeem the best deals available. The project’s goal was to blend convenience with smart functionality, ensuring that every interaction enhances the shopping experience while maximising savings.

INSIGHT DISCOVERY

User Research Findings

Our research highlighted key behaviours and preferences among deal-seekers, shaping the platform’s core functionality:

  • Ease of Access (50%) – Users prioritise a quick and intuitive way to find relevant coupons without unnecessary steps.
  • Personalised Offers (30%) – Many users prefer tailored deals based on their shopping habits, ensuring they see discounts that matter to them.
  • Multi-Platform Usage (20%) – A significant portion of users browse deals on mobile but complete purchases on desktop, emphasising the need for a seamless cross-platform experience.

Competitor Analysis

  • RetailMeNot – Offers an extensive coupon database but lacks personalization.
  • Honey – Provides automatic coupon application but is browser-extension focused.
  • Groupon – Features localized deals but requires upfront purchases.

Key Point

  • Pricing – Coupons Found provides free access to deals, unlike Groupon’s prepaid model.
  • User Experience – Designed for speed and ease, balancing automation with manual discovery.
  • Marketing Approach – Focuses on everyday savings, leveraging partnerships with retailers and targeted promotions.

DESIGN PROCESS

Information Architecture

We focused on designing an intuitive and seamless experience for users exploring available coupons. The structure was meticulously crafted to ensure users could easily navigate through the options, uncover discounts, and redeem offers with minimal effort. Each coupon section was strategically placed to enhance the user's shopping journey, encouraging engagement and providing an added value to their experience. The interface was designed to be clean and user-friendly, allowing for a smooth, effortless process from discovering to redeeming discounts.

Wireframes

The wireframes established the groundwork for a seamless and user-friendly design. Prioritizing clarity and efficiency, we created a structured layout that balances functionality with visual appeal. Every element was thoughtfully positioned to ensure a smooth navigation experience while maintaining a refined and modern aesthetic.

VISUAL AESTHETIC

Visual Showcase

A collection of high-quality visuals showcases the seamless experience of discovering and redeeming coupons. The clean design, intuitive layout, and carefully crafted details create a smooth and engaging journey, ensuring users can easily find the best deals with minimal effort.

FINAL IMPACT

Deliverables

This project was developed as part of my UI/UX course final assignment and is a conceptual design rather than a real-world implementation. The goal was to apply design principles, user research, and interaction design skills to create a polished and functional prototype. While not a commercial project, it reflects my ability to design engaging digital experiences from ideation to execution.

Client Feedback

The client was highly satisfied with the final design, praising the balance of aesthetics and functionality. They appreciated the clean, user-friendly interface and the intuitive layout that made coupon redemption effortless. The design captured the brand’s essence, offering a smooth, engaging experience that resonated with their target audience. The client highlighted how the simplicity and elegance of the platform would enhance user satisfaction and strengthen brand loyalty.
