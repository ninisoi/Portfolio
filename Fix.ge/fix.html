OVERVIEW

Role

UX Research, Design, Prototyping

Tools

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Figma

Date

2024

Fix.ge was designed to transform the way users access and manage repairs, offering a straightforward and efficient platform for all their repair needs. The vision was to create a seamless, user-friendly experience where individuals could easily find, request, and track repair services with minimal effort. The project aimed to combine convenience with intelligent functionality, ensuring that each interaction simplifies the repair process while delivering high-quality service to users.

INSIGHT DISCOVERY

User Research Findings

We found that users value a seamless, hassle-free experience when it comes to repair services. Key insights included:

  • Speed of Service: Many users expect quick response times and fast turnaround on repairs, emphasizing the need for efficient booking and service delivery.
  • Convenience: A large portion of users prefer services that come to their location, valuing the convenience of home or office repairs.
  • Cost Transparency: Users want clear, upfront pricing without hidden fees, ensuring there are no surprises during the booking or service process.

Competitor Analysis

Examining key competitors in the repair service space revealed several gaps and opportunities:

  • RepairPal offers a comprehensive service database but lacks personalization and localized suggestions.
  • Handy focuses on connecting users to professionals but can be cumbersome in terms of booking flexibility.
  • Angie’s List provides a wide range of service providers but often requires upfront payments, which can deter users.

DESIGN PROCESS

Information Architecture

We began by thoughtfully organizing the information architecture to ensure a smooth and user-friendly experience. The sitemap was designed to guide users through the process of exploring repair services, understanding the offerings, and making confident decisions. Each section was carefully placed to create an intuitive and seamless flow, from the homepage to individual service pages, ensuring users could easily navigate and find the solutions they needed. The structure was built to prioritize simplicity and efficiency, delivering an experience that felt natural and responsive at every step.

User Flow

The user flow was crafted with simplicity and efficiency as top priorities. We aimed to create an experience where users could easily navigate the platform, whether they were searching for a repair service, learning more about the offerings, or completing a booking. Clear, intuitive pathways were mapped out to ensure that the user journey was seamless and engaging, maintaining a consistent, professional feel throughout the platform. Each interaction was designed to align with the user’s needs, providing a smooth, hassle-free experience from start to finish.

VISUAL AESTHETIC

Visual Showcase

A collection of high-quality visuals showcases the seamless experience of discovering and redeeming coupons. The clean design, intuitive layout, and carefully crafted details create a smooth and engaging journey, ensuring users can easily find the best deals with minimal effort.

FINAL IMPACT

Deliverables

The final deliverables included a fully responsive repair service platform design, interactive prototypes, and comprehensive documentation for development. The design emphasized ease of use, professionalism, and clarity, ensuring a smooth and efficient user experience.

  • High-Fidelity Design: A refined, user-friendly interface that aligns with the platform's core values.
  • Interactive Prototypes: Simulated user journey to ensure smooth development and interaction flow.
  • Design Documentation: Detailed guidelines for a streamlined and efficient development process.

Client Feedback

The client was thoroughly pleased with the final design, particularly appreciating the balance of functionality and user-centric design. They highlighted the clean, intuitive interface that made service booking straightforward and effortless. The design effectively conveyed the brand’s professionalism, offering a seamless and engaging experience for users. The client also noted that the simplicity and elegance of the platform would enhance user satisfaction, build trust, and foster long-term loyalty.
